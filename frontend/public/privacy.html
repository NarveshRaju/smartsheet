Smartsheet → Calendar Sync

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Smartsheet → Calendar Sync connects a selected Smartsheet sheet to a selected Google Calendar. This policy describes the hosted app maintained through the NarveshRaju/smartsheet project.

Information we access and store

When you connect an account, we receive account identifiers, your email and display name, OAuth permissions, and access and refresh tokens. We store tokens encrypted in the app database. We read sheet metadata and row cells, calendar metadata and existing event details to synchronize events. Stored sync records can include row data, parsed event details, sheet and calendar identifiers, column mappings, timestamps and errors.

How information is used

We use this information to authenticate your accounts, map sheet rows to calendar events, create, update and remove synchronized entries, prevent duplicates and diagnose failures. Sync can run in the background while enabled. Google Calendar data is used to provide the calendar synchronization feature, not advertising or profiling. We do not sell personal data.

Service providers and AI processing

The hosted app uses Render for hosting, MongoDB Atlas for database storage and GitHub Actions for scheduled sync. Google and Smartsheet process requests to their respective services. These providers may process data needed to run the app, including operational logs.

When AI extraction is enabled and structured parsing cannot interpret a row, the app sends that row’s column names and cell values, mapping and timezone to the configured AI provider, currently Groq or an operator-configured Ollama server. This may include unmapped cells and personal information present in the row. Existing Google Calendar events and OAuth tokens are not included in AI extraction prompts. Provider processing is subject to its own policies. Only connect sheets you are authorized to process in this way.

Google API data

The app’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used to train generalized AI models.

Retention, access and deletion

Account information, configuration and sync records remain stored until removed by the operator; there is currently no automatic retention deadline. Disconnecting an account removes its stored OAuth credentials but does not automatically erase sync history or events already in Google Calendar. You can disable sync in the app and revoke Google access in your Google Account connections. Request removal of remaining app data using the contact method below. Provider backups and operational logs may remain according to their retention schedules.

Cookies and security

The app uses a session cookie to keep you signed in. It does not include advertising trackers. HTTPS and encrypted stored OAuth tokens help protect data, but no system can guarantee absolute security. Authorized operators may access stored data when necessary to support the service.

Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will appear on this page with a revised date.

For support, privacy questions or requests to delete stored app data, email navisharaju@gmail.com. Include the email associated with your connected account so the operator can verify the request. Never send passwords or OAuth tokens.
